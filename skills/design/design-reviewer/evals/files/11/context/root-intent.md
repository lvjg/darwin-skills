# Policy-guidance assistant intent

An internal employee should be able to ask which travel policy applies to a proposed trip.

- The applicable policy depends on employment country and request type.
- The answer must identify the policy ID and applicable scope, and must distinguish policy eligibility guidance from travel approval.
- If a decisive fact is missing or more than one policy matches, the assistant must ask for clarification rather than guess.
- Explanatory wording may vary while those decisions remain stable.
- The user asks whether the proposed Agent/Harness design is ready to implement.
